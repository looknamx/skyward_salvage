# Skyward Salvage — playable first version

An original side-view, turn-based artillery game for 2–4 friends in a private browser room. The visual direction follows the approved floating-island concept images in `../turn_artillery_concept/`.

## Rules

- Each of the four lobby reels includes a `?` random choice. All pending choices can be changed freely before marking ready, including scrolling past Random Mobile. Random Mobile retains the 5% Aegis chance; other rolls choose equally among the ten ordinary Mobiles. Each equipment reel independently rolls rare gold with 5% probability; the remaining 95% is divided equally among attack, defense, and health. Each gold piece gives +3 attack, +3 defense, and +3 HP, stacking across three slots. Gold can only be rolled, not selected directly. Results resolve when gameplay starts. Opponents cannot inspect equipment choices or pending random equipment in the lobby. Rematch clears all random choices.
- The Wind Mobile category contains Gale and Tempest. At absolute wind strength >= 7, they gain +12 damage on normal and special shots before the Double Damage multiplier. Gale has 100 HP and normal damage 28; Tempest has 100 HP and normal damage 30. Their transparent ImageGen artwork and generation prompts are documented in `art_review/wind-mobiles.md`.

- One player creates a room and shares its code or link. In the lobby each player chooses from the ten ordinary Mobiles or commits to a random Mobile in the car reel. Guests mark themselves ready; the host starts when 2–4 players have joined and all guests are ready. Changing the mode or joining the room clears guest readiness.
- Players act in join order. A turn lasts 30 seconds. A timed-out turn passes to the next living player.
- Move with the left and right arrow keys or A/D at 88 world units per second, up to 175 world units of cumulative walking per turn, following the ground. Mobiles can walk through one another. Walking faces the Mobile in that direction; R flips it in place. The server owns position and facing and broadcasts both to every client.
- Adjust the 10–80 degree aim from the Mobile's front with the up and down arrow keys or W/S; the angle stays at its last setting on the next turn. The HUD number and dial show the resulting muzzle elevation above horizontal after terrain tilt, while the slider retains the Mobile-relative setting. Hold Fire or Space to charge power smoothly from 5% to 100% over 2.4 seconds, then release to shoot. The shot starts at the front of the Mobile. On each new turn wind has a 20% chance to change and accelerates shots horizontally. There is no trajectory preview; the server computes the actual shot.
- Loom fires a precise thread orb with a moderate blast. Manta and Kestrel fire one parent projectile that splits on ground contact into two bouncing fragments (three with Special). Equipment ATK is divided as evenly as possible among fragments, so the total bonus per shot remains equal to the equipped bonus; a parent striking a Mobile before the ground keeps the full bonus and explodes without splitting. Double Damage multiplies each resulting fragment after the split. Every Mobile has the same direct-hit box, and fragments can also collide with it. Borer fires a heavy drill shell with a wider crater. Vesper's energy orb resists wind, and its special ignores wind entirely. Bramble's seed mortar has a moderate blast and its special also restores 22 HP to itself. Explosions damage players within a radius and deform the ground; Mobiles settle onto the new surface. Each Mobile has 1 or 2 base defense, applied to every damaging shell before equipment defense.
- After attack equipment, wind bonuses, Double Damage, falloff, and base plus equipment DEF are resolved, remaining Mobile shot damage is halved and rounded per shell. Lightning adds its fixed +5 afterward. Meteor damage and falling deaths keep their existing values.
- The island underside is fixed when a round starts. Repeated excavation can remove a column all the way through it. Unsupported Mobiles fall below the playfield and immediately lose their remaining HP; walking into an opening has the same effect. Shot-induced falling deaths count toward the shooter's damage and hit statistics. Dropped items on destroyed ground disappear. Practice resets the arena if either practice position loses its supporting ground.
- In the lobby, each player may choose a pending random Mobile or change back to a specific ordinary Mobile before readying up. The lobby shows only that the roll is pending. After the host starts the round, the server uses a secure roll with a 5% chance of Aegis, a rare 150 HP Mobile, and reveals the result to everyone in the gameplay state. Other rolls choose one of the ten ordinary 100 HP Mobiles. Aegis cannot be selected directly.
- Each player begins with one of each item. Double boosts the next shot and can be armed during the turn. Repair restores 28 HP and spends the turn. Teleport arms a WARP projectile using the same aim and power controls as a normal shot. The Mobile moves to its unoccupied ground impact point and spends the turn.
- A player at zero HP is out. The last living player wins in free for all mode. In 2v2, alternating join order assigns teams and teammates cannot damage each other. The last living team wins. If all remaining players are eliminated by one shot, the round is a draw.
- Each Mobile begins each round with one special shot: Loom trades blast radius for precise damage, Manta splits into three shots, Borer makes a larger blast, Vesper ignores wind, Bramble heals itself, and Aegis fires a stronger blast. Every eighth turn drops a random item: Double, Repair, Teleport and Double Play each have a 23% chance, while a Special refill has an 8% chance. A player can collect the Special refill only after spending their current special shot, and the other drops only when the matching item slot is empty. Normal and special shots can destroy a dropped item by direct impact or blast radius.
- Players disconnected for up to 45 seconds can resume using a browser session token. Their active turn advances immediately. After the grace period, they are eliminated from an active match. When all connected players vote to rematch, the same room returns to its lobby with fresh Mobile choices and guest readiness; the host starts the next round after everyone is ready.
- The map theme and terrain seed are randomized on every start. The first version has Cloud Reef, Clockwork Orchard, and Glass Dunes.

## Technical scope

- Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite for the 2D playfield; HTML/CSS for lobby and HUD.
- Node.js + WebSocket server owns rooms, turn timer, terrain, physics, damage, items, and victory. Browser clients send actions and render snapshots.
- One Node process holds rooms and match statistics in memory. The result screen shows player statistics. There are no accounts, matchmaking, persistent records, or public deployment in this version.
- WebSocket upgrades validate origin; commands have payload and rate limits; ping/pong removes stale sockets. `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` can restrict deployment origins explicitly.
- Generated backgrounds, transparent Mobile sprites, item icons, and rock textures are rendered in Phaser. The server's numeric terrain and hit detection remain deterministic and independent of those images.
- Code-drawn projectile silhouettes and palettes distinguish each Mobile. Special shots add larger energy rings and impact rays; destroyed Mobiles get a short debris burst. Each clear turn has a 10% chance to spawn a vertical lane at a randomized field position, with lightning, storm, and rain equally likely. The lane lasts four turns, has no label above it, and cannot be overwritten. When a projectile crosses the lane and damages a Mobile, each target independently has a 70% chance of the weather effect: lightning adds +5 damage, storm ignores the target's base and equipment DEF without changing the projectile trajectory, and rain prevents item and special use during the target's next active turn. Missed lanes leave projectile rules unchanged.

## Art

The approved look uses layered floating islands, luminous rims, clouds, and waterfalls. ImageGen produced three background plates, three matching rock textures, five transparent Mobile cutouts, and three item icons. The playfield clips rock textures to the authoritative terrain shape and adds decorative water, foliage, and surface lighting without affecting collisions.

## Double Play and practice selection

- Slot 4 is Double Play; slot 5 is Special. Keyboard numbers 1–5 and Numpad 1–5 match slot order. Each player starts with one Double Play. Activation arms exactly one extra consecutive turn; finishing the current turn by firing, repairing, warping or timeout consumes that extra turn. Time and movement budget reset as usual. Death, disconnect, or match completion take precedence.
- Practice permits selecting any Mobile, including Aegis, before entry or inside Settings. Switching restores the same seeded arena and resets inventory and stats. It stays private and untimed.
- Wind and turn have separate glass plaques; the countdown uses a cyan circular progress ring and a warm warning color in the last five seconds. Panel opacity stays at 35%.
