# Wind Mobile asset notes

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-29. Style references: `public/assets/characters/loom.png` and `halo.png`. The generated originals remain in the Codex generated-images folder; shipping copies are in `public/assets/characters/gale.png` and `tempest.png`. Both are transparent RGBA PNGs with the same canvas size as existing Mobile art.

## Gameplay

- Gale: mint and ivory turbine buggy, 100 base HP, normal damage 28, special damage 40; broader normal blast (radius 56).
- Tempest: violet storm crawler, 100 base HP, normal damage 30, special damage 43; narrower normal blast (radius 48).
- Both belong to the Wind category and receive +12 damage when absolute wind strength is at least 7. This applies to normal and special shots, before Double Damage; equipment and defense follow existing rules. Normal projectile wind physics are retained.
- Both can be selected in the car reel, rolled as ordinary Mobiles, and selected in practice.
