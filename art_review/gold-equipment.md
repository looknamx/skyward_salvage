# Rare solar gold equipment

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-29 using the corresponding defense equipment PNGs as style/layout references. Transparent PNG assets are saved in `public/assets/equipment/gold-hat.png`, `gold-armor.png`, and `gold-flag.png`. Generated originals remain in the Codex generated-images folder.

Each equipment slot independently has a 5% chance of gold when its Random choice resolves at gameplay start. The other 95% is divided equally among the three ordinary sets. Every gold piece adds +3 ATK, +3 DEF and +3 maximum HP; three pieces total +9 in each stat. Gold is not directly selectable and remains private until gameplay like the other equipment.

The wind damage bonus remains in combat calculations, but the extra +12 HUD label, Wind category in practice dropdowns, and the description below the slot machine are removed. Random Mobile no longer locks browsing; leaving it selects a specific vehicle and cancels the pending roll.
